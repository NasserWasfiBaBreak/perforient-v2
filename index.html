07NotesFrequently asked questions

Questions, answered

Straight answers about how process optimization with PERFORIENT works.

N-01What is a free process audit?

We walk through the process that is costing your business the most, estimate how much time and money it is leaking, and outline clear next steps. There is no cost and no obligation.

N-02What kinds of businesses do you work with?

We work with small and mid-sized businesses. Our published results come from engineering and education, but the method applies to any business with repeatable workflows, such as approvals, onboarding, enrollment, order processing, or reporting.

N-03Do you just give recommendations?

No. We map your operations, redesign the workflow, choose the right tools, and implement the change with your team. You get a working process, not a recommendation deck.

N-04How long does an engagement take?

It depends on the scope of the problem. For example, our engineering firm engagement ran 3 to 6 months. We agree on scope and timeline with you after the audit.

N-05How do you measure results?

We measure a baseline before changing anything, such as cycle time, processing time per case, or daily capacity, and then track the same metrics after implementation. That is how we report results like an 85% shorter R&D cycle or 3x enrollment capacity.

N-06Do you work remotely or on-site?

We are remote-first, with on-site intervention when the problem needs boots on the ground.

N-07What happens after the project ends?

We document everything and restructure how your team works so the new process runs without us.
